## Rule: neuron permutation for every embedded net

When a new network (policy or value) is trained and it replaces an embedded net, always compute its neuron permutation and embed it with the net (user, 2026-11-24).

**Why:** the engine skips zero 4-byte blocks of the accumulator output in layer_one (SparseLayer). Grouping co-active neurons cuts non-zero blocks: value net 104 -> 71 of 512 (-32 %, -9 % value forward instructions, bit-exact); policy net 45.6 -> 36.2 of 256 (small gain). A permutation only fits the net it was computed for.

**How to apply:** `Training/scripts/compute_permutation.py` (value nets: positions from a `--log_evals` search log, e.g. /mnt/c/TrainingData/evallog/search20ms.samples; policy nets: POL1 validation file). Output raw size_t[2*L1]. Engine: INCBIN the .perm file and call `<net>.load_permutation_from_array(...)` BEFORE `<net>.load_from_array(...)` (commented lines exist in main.cpp; check CheckerBoard.cpp too). Verify bench node counts are identical with and without the permutation. Test options: `--value_perm FILE`, `--policy_perm FILE` (with --policy_file). Related: the first section.
